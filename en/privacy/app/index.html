1. What this document covers

Mitti GO is an app for parents (Android, iOS, Android TV). The parent chooses YouTube channels and playlists, and the child watches only those in a simple kids' interface. This policy explains how data is handled in the Mitti GO app and on the mitti-go.uz website.

By using the app you agree to this policy and to the Terms of Use. The policy follows the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data” and the rules of Google Play, the App Store and the YouTube API Services.

2. In short

  • We collect nothing about your child: the child has no profile — no name, photo or age.
  • What the child watched, their progress, searches and screen time stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.
  • The PIN never leaves the device and is well protected.
  • A Google account is optional. It is needed only for PIN recovery, Pro and linked devices.
  • The app has no ads of its own, no third-party analytics and no advertising IDs. We don't sell data.

3. What stays on the device only

This data is created and kept on your phone, tablet or TV. Our server never receives it:

  • allowed channels and playlists, hidden playlists, Favorites and settings (language, theme, Shorts, autoplay);
  • watch history, video progress and “Keep watching”, the child's searches;
  • screen time: how long the child watched today, the limit and bedtime;
  • the PIN and the wrong-attempt counter — in the device's secure storage.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. The PIN and security data are not restored from a device backup.

4. What our server receives

The Mitti GO server fetches public YouTube data, checks your plan and, if you want, connects an account. It receives:

  • An anonymous install ID — a random key the server uses to limit how often requests are made. It is not linked to your account and contains no device data.
  • YouTube requests — IDs of public channels, playlists and videos, and the search text when a parent looks for a channel. These requests are not linked to an account.
  • Your Google account (if you connect it) — your Google ID, e-mail address and name, when the account was created and when you last signed in, and which edition of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you accepted and when.
  • Plan and promo codes — your Pro status and its end date, promo-code and trial activations. So that a code or the trial can't be used twice, a mark that it was used is kept for the account and the device. The trial mark is kept as hashes and stays after the account is deleted, so the trial can't be taken again.
  • Support requests (if you write to us from the app) — the request number, your Google account's e-mail address (the reply goes there), the topic and text, your plan and activated promo codes, and, if you leave the box ticked, the app version, platform and mode and which device it is about (name, model, parent phone or child device). Letters you send to our e-mail addresses are kept in the Mitti GO mailbox.
  • IP address — the server sees it with every request and uses it only in memory to limit how often sign-ins, device linking, promo codes and registrations can be tried. The IP address is not stored in the database.
  • Your account's devices (if you connect an account) — every parent phone signed in with the account and every linked child device is recorded as a separate device: its role (parent phone or child device), name, model and system version (for example, “Samsung Galaxy A15 · Android 14”), platform, when it was added and when it last contacted the server, and a random device key — stored only as a hash. These records are used to count the devices in your plan and to build the Devices list in the app. The key is made by the app itself, is not linked to advertising or system identifiers and says nothing about what the child watches.
  • Linked devices (if you use them) — the family settings that have to reach the child's device (allowed channels and playlists, hidden playlists, Favorites, Shorts and playback settings, each device's screen time), the child device's access key and a one-time linking code valid for 10 minutes.

The server never receives watch history, progress, the child's searches, screen-time usage, the PIN or anything the child types.

5. YouTube and Google services

Mitti GO uses the YouTube API Services. Videos play in YouTube's official player (youtube-nocookie.com), unchanged: YouTube itself shows its logo, captions and ads in the player.

The YouTube player and Google may store cookies and similar data on the device and process playback information under their own rules — see the Google Privacy Policy. By using Mitti GO you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Sign-in with Google is handled by Google. You can remove Mitti GO's access to your account on the Google account “Third-party apps” page. Mitti GO gets no access to your YouTube channel, subscriptions or history.

The child can't open links from the player to other sites: the parent's PIN is needed first.

6. Payments

Pro is purchased through Google Play (and the App Store once the app is there). The store takes the payment. Mitti GO receives only the purchase and subscription period; we never see or store card details.

Promo codes can be bought from Mitti GO under an agreement. For such a purchase we process what the agreement and accounting and tax records require: the buyer's name and details, a contact person and payment information. These records are kept as long as the law of the Republic of Uzbekistan requires.

7. Children

Mitti GO is an app for parents. An adult installs and sets it up; the child only watches what the parent allowed. The child creates no account and enters no personal data. Search in the kids' area runs on the device and is not sent over the network.

Purchases, sign-in, external links and the parent area are protected by the PIN. If you believe we received a child's data by mistake, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Why we process data

  • to make the app work: show allowed videos and refresh channels;
  • to recover the PIN and link Pro to an account;
  • to pass settings to linked child devices;
  • to count the devices in your plan and show you where you are signed in and which child devices are linked;
  • to answer support requests;
  • to protect the service from abuse: rate limits and one-time use of codes and the trial;
  • to keep a record that you accepted the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy;
  • to meet legal duties: records of payments under agreements and taxes.

9. Who receives data

We don't sell or rent data. Data goes only to those the service can't run without:

  • Google — Sign-in with Google, the YouTube API Services, Google Play;
  • Apple — the App Store, once the app is there;
  • the hosting provider in the Republic of Uzbekistan whose servers run Mitti GO and hold our mailboxes;
  • public authorities — only when the law requires it.

The Mitti GO server and its backups are in the Republic of Uzbekistan: personal data is stored on the territory of Uzbekistan, as Article 27¹ of the Law “On Personal Data” requires. It goes abroad only to Google and Apple, as far as Sign-in with Google, YouTube and the app stores need it.

10. How long data is kept

  • your account — until you delete it;
  • sign-in tokens — no longer than 60 days after last use;
  • public YouTube data — deleted if not refreshed for 30 days;
  • a parent phone's record — until you sign out on that phone (or from the Devices list); a phone unused for 60 days stops taking a place in the plan;
  • a child device's record — until you unlink it or delete the account;
  • promo-code records — until the code's validity ends;
  • the trial mark (hashes) — without a time limit, so the trial can't be taken again;
  • support requests — as long as they are needed to answer and solve the issue; we delete them earlier on request;
  • agreement and payment records — as long as the law of the Republic of Uzbekistan requires.

11. Your rights and deleting data

You can ask what data we hold about you, correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent. Write to us — the contacts are below.

How to delete data:

  • your account: in the app, Profile → Account → Delete account — the account, tokens, the account's devices and the family settings are deleted from the server and linked devices are disconnected. The trial mark (as hashes) and support requests stay — we delete them on request;
  • data on the device: uninstall the app;
  • a subscription is cancelled in the store: Google Play → Subscriptions. Deleting the account doesn't cancel a store subscription.

12. Security

We protect data with organisational and technical measures: the connection to the server is encrypted, access keys and codes are protected, and the PIN never leaves the device. Parent screens are kept out of the app-switcher snapshots. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

We update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows when the current version took effect. We'll tell you about significant changes in the app or on the website.
