How Mitti-GO began

Two fathers of wonderful daughters started thinking about how modern technology shapes the way kids grow up. Their children loved watching YouTube, but the parents wanted more peace of mind, more control and more confidence in what their child was seeing.

How it worksarrow_forward Plansarrow_forward

Our story in three steps

  1. The problem

    Kids drift easily from one video to the next, and algorithms show them anything.

  2. arrow_forward

  3. The idea

    Build a video player for kids where parents choose the content, not random recommendations.

  4. arrow_forward

  5. The solution

    Mitti GO helps create a safe video space for your child.

Our story

We didn't want to ban technology for kids altogether. We wanted a simple, clear tool that helps families choose good content, cut out random videos and give children a safer way to watch.

Step by step, that's how Mitti GO — an app made by parents, for parents — came to be.
