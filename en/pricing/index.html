How limits work

block

Hit the limit

You can't add more, but you can still manage everything: hide, star, remove. Or pick separate playlists instead of a whole channel.

pause_circle

If Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. You get 7 days to choose which 5 channels and 10 playlists stay. The rest are paused and come back when you renew.

visibility_off

Hidden playlists don't count

Hide a playlist in an added channel and it disappears for your child — and doesn't count toward the limit.

star

Stars are free

A Favorite puts a channel first for your child. It grants no access and doesn't count toward limits.

Questions about plans

Do I have to pay to use Mitti GO?add

No. The free plan includes up to 5 channels, 10 playlists and every child-safety feature. Pro is only for families who want to manage Screen Time.

How much will Pro cost?add

We'll announce Pro pricing and limits when the app launches on Google Play and the App Store.

Is Screen Time part of the free plan?add

No, Screen Time is part of Pro. On the free plan nothing is counted and nothing is blocked.

What happens to my channels if Pro ends?add

Nothing is deleted. You get 7 days to choose which 5 channels and 10 playlists stay. The rest are paused and come back when you renew.

Will my child know we're on a free plan or hit a limit?add

No. Your child never sees the words “Pro” or “limit”. When time is up, they see a gentle screen with the sleeping mascot.
