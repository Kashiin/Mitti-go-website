5 simple questions: should you allow this channel?

Mitti GO lets you add up to 5 whole channels for free, so it pays to choose well. Here's what to check before you allow a channel.

Before allowing a channel, watch a couple of its videos yourself and ask five questions.

1

Which age is it for?

Pace, vocabulary and topics should suit your child.

  • Simple, clear language
  • Calm pace
  • Age-appropriate topics: colours, animals, songs, everyday situations
2

How often are new videos posted?

New videos from an allowed channel appear for your child on their own. Check the channel's Videos tab: how often, and what exactly gets posted.

3

Any clickbait?

Loud thumbnails and titles are a bad sign.

4

Do all the playlists fit?

If a channel mixes different kinds of playlists, check them. Hide the extra ones or allow only the playlists you need.

5

Does your child enjoy it?

Even the most useful channel won't work if it's boring. Good signs:

  • Watches with interest
  • Asks for more
  • Calmly watches to the end

Start with 2–3 channels and watch for a week. You can add more any time in Profile.
