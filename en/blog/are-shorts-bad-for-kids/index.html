Why Shorts are off by default in Mitti-GO

An endless feed of short videos and a child's attention don't mix well. Here's why — and how Mitti GO handles it.

Short vertical videos are built to make you watch "just one more". For an adult that's a lost half hour; for a child, stopping is even harder.

What Shorts are

Short vertical videos that replace each other automatically in an endless feed.

timerUp to 3 minutesvery short clips
all_inclusiveEndless feednew videos keep coming
psychologyHighly engaginghold attention longer than needed

How Shorts can harm kids

  • psychology
    Weaker focusIt gets harder to stay with one activity for long.
  • bedtime
    Broken routinesIt's easy to watch longer than planned.
  • bolt
    Hooked on quick thrillsRegular, longer videos start to feel boring.
  • sms_failed
    Unwanted contentEven Shorts can include scenes you'd rather your child didn't see.

How it works in Mitti-GO

  1. Shorts are off from the start. Your child doesn't even see a Shorts tab.
  2. If you turn them on, only Shorts from allowed channels appear — no shared feed, no random videos.
  3. Switch them on or off in Profile → Shorts, and for each channel separately.
Parents should make these decisions

In Mitti GO you decide what your child watches. No pushy algorithms, no endless feed — just a conscious choice.
