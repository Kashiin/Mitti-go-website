How the Mitti-GO channel whitelist works

You choose channels and playlists — your child watches only what's approved. How to add a channel, allow just a playlist, hide what you don't want and protect the settings.

A whitelist is a list of channels and playlists you've approved. In Mitti-GO that's all your child sees: no recommendations from across YouTube, no random channels and no “one more video” from the algorithm.

It sounds simple, but the details matter: you can allow a whole channel or just one playlist, hide what you don't want and lock the settings with a PIN. Here's how it works, step by step.

Whitelist vs blacklist: what's the difference

block
Blacklist

Everything is open except what you've blocked. New and unfamiliar content reaches your child before you get a chance to check it.

verified_user
Whitelist

Everything is closed except what you've allowed. A new channel never appears on its own — only after you decide.

info

Mitti-GO works only as a whitelist: it isn't an extra mode, it's the foundation of the app. How that differs from YouTube Kids — see “How is Mitti-GO different from YouTube Kids?”.

How to build a good whitelist

  1. Start with 3–5 channels. A short list is easier to check and easier for your child to choose from.
  2. Big channel? Use playlists. Popular creators often have videos for different ages: allow only the playlists that fit.
  3. Watch a couple of videos yourself. A quick way to check a channel — “5 simple questions”.
  4. Star the favourites. Favorite channels come first for your child — a star grants no access and doesn't count toward limits.
  5. Review the list once a week. See what's new and hide what you don't want.

Not sure where to start? Here's a ready-made list: “10 safe YouTube channels for preschoolers”.

Common questions

Can I allow or block a single video?add

No — only channels and playlists. It's easier to keep track, and new videos arrive on their own. If a channel has a playlist you don't want, hide it.

What if an unsuitable video appears on an approved channel?add

Hide the playlist it's in, or remove the channel entirely — the video disappears for your child right away. For more control, allow only separate playlists from that creator.

Will my child see that a channel was hidden or removed?add

No. Anything that's no longer allowed simply disappears — no “Blocked by your parent” messages.

How many channels can I add for free?add

Up to 5 whole channels and up to 10 separate playlists. Playlists of added channels, hiding and Favorites are unlimited. More on the Plans page.
